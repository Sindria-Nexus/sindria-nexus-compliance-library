> **Last verified:** 21 November 2026. **Next check:** 18 February 2027.

## How to report an error

Open an issue at
[github.com/Sindria-Nexus/sindria-nexus-compliance-library/issues](https://github.com/Sindria-Nexus/sindria-nexus-compliance-library/issues).

## What a correction needs

A correction is actionable when it gives all four of these:

1. **The law** — which of the seven laws it concerns.
2. **The section** — the exact section number (e.g. Civ. Code §3111,
   BPC §22757.3, 11 CCR §7221).
3. **An official link** — a link to the official source (leginfo, a
   California agency page, the OAL register, a court filing). A secondary
   source alone is not enough to close an open item, though it can point
   the way to the official one.
4. **The date you checked it** — the date you read the source.

Corrections that name a section without an official link, or that rest on
a secondary source alone, are recorded as open items rather than applied.

## What happens next

Corrections are reviewed within a few business days. Accepted corrections
are applied in the next version and recorded in
[CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md). If a correction changes a screening result
or a mapping, the change is noted in the changelog entry.

## What not to send

- Do not send legal advice or ask for it in an issue — this preview is not
  a legal service.
- Do not send text copied from ISO/IEC 42001 or any other paid standard.
- Do not send personal data.
